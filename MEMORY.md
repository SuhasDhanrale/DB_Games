# Dainik Bhaskar Games - Core Design Principles
*Extracted from GBL 2026 - Game Design Brief*

## 1. The Core Philosophy: Moods, Not Menus
*   **"We are not making five different games. We are making five reasons to play every day."**
*   The hub shouldn't just present a standard menu of games. We should frame the platform around the *player's mood* (e.g., Nostalgia, Brain Challenge, Discovery, Fun/Excitement).
*   Build one daily product in which each game answers a different human need.

## 2. The Product Loop & Audience
*   **Loop:** Open App -> Pick a Mood -> Play 1-3 minutes -> See Result -> Come back tomorrow.
*   **Audience:** The games need to be extremely lightweight, designed for Indian non-gamers and casual players who are already inside a news ecosystem. 
*   **Goal:** Habit-driven but low-commitment engagement.

## 3. Engagement & Progression (No Grinding)
*   **No spam or heavy meta-games:** Explicitly avoid coins, gems, reward spam, and heavy monetization loops. Keep the system light.
*   **Focus on Mastery & Streaks:** Progression should be simple and meaningful. Use signals like daily streaks, personal bests, solve rates, or accuracy/time. 
*   **Principle:** The player gets better and discovers more. The system rewards mastery and curiosity, not grinding.

## 4. Meaningful Indian Relevance
*   Localization goes beyond just the interface language.
*   The content itself (places, food, festivals, cultural elements) should be regionally recognizable to drive curiosity and connection.
*   **Rule:** Avoid stereotypes; the goal is recognition + curiosity.
