Why Games?
We believe games can become a powerful part of the daily digital experience, creating habit, 
engagement, entertainment, and meaningful interaction at scale.
But much of the gaming world has been shaped by cultures, behaviours, and design philosophies 
that evolved elsewhere. India has its own rhythms, references, social dynamics, and ways of 
playing, and we believe there is still enormous room to design games around them.
Dainik Bhaskar reaches millions of people across India every day. That gives us a unique 
opportunity to explore what games can become when they are designed not just for gamers, but 
for everyday people, everyday habits, and everyday moments of play.
We are building a team of exceptional game-makers with one ambition: to create a breakout 
game built in India, for India.
