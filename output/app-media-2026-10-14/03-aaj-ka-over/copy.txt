Aaj Ka Over / आज का ओवर

Tagline: Six balls. One challenge. Your moment.

Description: Chase or defend a cricket target in six balls.

How to play: Pick a team and choose batting or bowling. When batting, swipe toward your shot as the ball reaches the green timing mark. When bowling, choose a spot on the pitch and release. Chase or defend the target within six balls; two wickets end the innings.
