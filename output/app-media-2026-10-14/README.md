# Dainik Bhaskar — App media pack

Created 14 October 2026 for the five games in the current collection.

- Open **index.html** for the visual gallery, descriptions, taglines, play instructions and copy buttons.
- **COPY-PASTE.txt** contains all English copy; each game folder also has **copy.txt**.
- **00-collection-cover.png** is the overall cover page, with all five front screenshots. Its editable HTML source is included.
- **01-collection-home.png** is the shared home screen.
- In every game folder, **00-cover.png** is the actual front screenshot to use as that game's cover. Remaining PNGs show instructions, gameplay and outcomes or other play modes.
- **app-copy.json** provides structured copy and screenshot captions.
- **capture-manifest.json** records capture metadata.

## Image sizes

Mobile captures are 1170 × 2532 PNG (390 × 844 viewport at 3×). The collection cover is 1920 × 1080 PNG. Screenshots are unretouched captures of the current local prototype; the overall cover arranges their originals.

## Capture context

Edition 1 (d1). The project cycles among authored demo editions. The screenshots do not establish a live daily publishing service or independently verify displayed news or match claims. Results were reached through actual interface actions; there are no injected scores or fabricated completion states. The Niti card shows a sample name. The five games are the ones registered in the current home, rather than older standalone experiments also present in the repository.
