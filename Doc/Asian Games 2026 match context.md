User-selected tournament: the **2026 Asian Games**, not the Asia Cup or World Cup. Research checked on 11 October 2026. Two verified men's matches anchor the prototype.

These were regular T20 matches, not tie-breaking Super Overs. Retain the working game title “सुपर ओवर” but identify its format as a six-ball practice challenge inspired by the match. The existing 18-run batting and 15-run bowling targets are authored gameplay settings, **not historical final-over equations**. The two-wicket rule is also a practice rule.

Show “आपका खेल” and “असली मैच” separately. Keep original demo article summaries short, label them as sourced demo material, display match dates, and offer the official source. Do not invent ball-by-ball events or portray the player's outcome as a real match result.

This decision supersedes the 2024 World Cup and fictional-practice cricket directions in the earlier design documents. Other games' editorial packs are outside this change.
