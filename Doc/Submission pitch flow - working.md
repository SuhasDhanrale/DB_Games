## Daily engagement argument and development boundary

The organizer brief, p. 9, asks for mass appeal, simple mechanics that can deepen with mastery, Indian relevance, and daily engagement with long-term evolution through systems. Page 18 excludes reward-only models with weak gameplay and heavy monetization/complexity.

- **Novelty:** authored words, thoughts, verified pairs and cricket situations can change; Carrom changes its layout. Publishing checked daily/current/local packs needs a production editorial workflow.
- **Mastery:** fewer guesses, better timing and fewer shots make improvement understandable. Reflection need not have a leaderboard. A deeper challenge sequence or difficulty adaptation is future design, not current functionality.
- **Continuity:** the implemented shared participation history and mashal connect days. The latest `Doc/Mashal journey.md` says consecutive-day streaks and cumulative participation levels are separate; gaps can reset a streak while preserving earned levels. Switching demo editions does not count as multiple days.
- **Social continuation:** Niti card creation exists. Hosted return links, friend challenges and reliable share-to-play acquisition are future integration.
- **Validation:** judge the hypothesis by first starts, completions, later voluntary returns, relevant article engagement, clarity, and editorial workload. No numerical retention or revenue claim.
