# Opening and news journey revision

Design decisions implemented on 11 October 2026 following user review:

1. **Opening:** retain “खबर से खेल तक, खेल से समझ तक।” from the pitch revision. Show five original animated SVGs: flipping Hindi tiles, a thought card being written, a cricket shot, a Carrom pocket, and a Jodo connection. The opening lasts about 2.6 seconds, can be skipped immediately, supports reduced motion, and can be replayed from the hub.
2. **Navigation:** combine language, audio and reset controls with profile. Replace the separate settings navigation slot with “समाचार”, linking to the local demo feed. Keep the selected demo edition in links. Existing `/settings` routes open profile for compatibility.
3. **News connection:** let readers enter a related game from its article. The new cricket stories select the matching opponent in the game; “आज के खेल” returns to the collection. Article sources and match dates remain visible.
4. **Cricket:** use the user's clarified **2026 Asian Games** context, with the Pakistan final and Sri Lanka semifinal. See [the researched match note](Asian%20Games%202026%20match%20context.md). Preserve the existing batting/bowling gameplay and authored 18/15-run practice settings. Show real outcomes separately from player results.

These choices supersede the older separate-settings navigation and 2024 World Cup cricket scenarios. Other editorial and progression review findings remain separate work.
